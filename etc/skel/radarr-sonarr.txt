ARR applications
----------------
The server keeps the latest Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, Lidarr, and Readarr
builds in /opt/Radarr, /opt/Sonarr, /opt/Prowlarr, /opt/Lidarr, and
/opt/Readarr. Use the `arrinfo` shell alias (already in your profile) to see
the HTTPS URLs proxied through nginx:

    arrinfo

Each Arr app has its own authentication, separate from your seedbox panel.
User-local installs from install-media-stack.sh pre-set Forms authentication
and write the per-app URLs, usernames, and generated passwords to:

    ~/.media-stack-credentials.txt

Credentials are per-app; setting or changing them in one app does not configure
the others. If you later change a password inside an app, update your own
records because the installer will preserve existing app auth on reruns.

Restart and update behavior depends on installation type: PMSS maintains the
system-wide /opt apps during updates, while user-local installs from
install-media-stack.sh are managed from your shell aliases.
