ARR applications
----------------
The server keeps the latest Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, Lidarr, and Readarr
builds in /opt/Radarr, /opt/Sonarr, /opt/Prowlarr, /opt/Lidarr, and
/opt/Readarr. Use the `arrinfo` shell alias (already in your profile) to see
the HTTPS URLs proxied through nginx:

    arrinfo

Each Arr app has its own authentication, separate from your seedbox panel. Set
up Forms authentication inside each app before sharing or relying on its URL.
Until Forms auth is configured, Arr apps may be reachable to anyone who knows
the proxied URL. Credentials are per-app; setting them in one app does not
configure the others.

Restart and update behavior depends on installation type: PMSS maintains the
system-wide /opt apps during updates, while user-local installs from
install-media-stack.sh are managed from your shell aliases.
