ARR applications
----------------
The server keeps the latest Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, Lidarr, and Readarr
builds in /opt/Radarr, /opt/Sonarr, /opt/Prowlarr, /opt/Lidarr, and
/opt/Readarr. Use the `arrinfo` shell alias (already in your profile) to see
the HTTPS URLs proxied through nginx:

    arrinfo

Login with your panel credentials unless you have changed them in the web UI.
Restart your instances via the control panel if needed—system-wide services are
managed by PMSS during updates.
