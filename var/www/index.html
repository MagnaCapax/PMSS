Pulsed Media Seedbox Server

This is a Pulsed Media seedbox server. Each seedbox on it has its own address.

Your seedbox is at https://this-server/user-YOURUSERNAME/
Use the username from your welcome e-mail in place of YOURUSERNAME.

Where is my login?

  1. The welcome e-mail sent when your seedbox was set up has your direct link, username and password.
  2. Or log in to the client area, open My Services, choose the seedbox and press Open seedbox.
