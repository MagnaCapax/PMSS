502 - Bad Gateway

The sage is reconnecting the pathways.

Common reasons for this page include:

  • Your web service is restarting after a temporary crash.
  • Your disk quota is full and the backend cannot write new files.
  • Your account is suspended and needs billing attention.
  • Temporary server-wide storage pressure is delaying recovery.

Wait 1-2 minutes and retry.

If your disk quota is full, connect with SFTP and delete files to free space.

If this continues for more than a few minutes, open a support ticket.
